# Fear Street Trilogy

![Fear Street Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/1/16/Fear_Street_Trilogy_poster.jpg)

**Genre:** Horror, Mystery, Supernatural  
*Directed by Leigh Janiak*

## Overview
> The *Fear Street Trilogy* is a series of horror films on Netflix that reveal the dark history of a cursed town called Shadyside.  
> The curse has lasted for centuries — and it all connects back to a witch named **Sarah Fier**.

### Movies in the Trilogy
1. *Fear Street Part One: 1994*  
2. *Fear Street Part Two: 1978*  
3. *Fear Street Part Three: 1666*

- **Main Characters**
  - Deena Johnson  
  - Samantha Fraser  
  - Sarah Fier  

`"You can’t stop the darkness. But maybe, you can end it."`
